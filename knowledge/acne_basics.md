# Acne care basics

## Acne care principles
Mild acne care is gentle: a non-harsh cleanser twice a day, an oil-free or gel moisturizer, daily sunscreen, and one proven acne ingredient such as salicylic acid, benzoyl peroxide, adapalene or azelaic acid. Results take six to eight weeks, so give a routine time before changing it.

## Common mistakes
Over-washing, scrubbing, picking pimples and using many strong actives together damage the skin barrier and often make acne and redness worse. Skipping moisturizer on oily skin can also increase oil and irritation.

## Blackheads and whiteheads
Blackheads and whiteheads (closed comedones) are clogged pores. Salicylic acid, adapalene and azelaic acid are commonly used. Regular gentle exfoliation a few times a week can help. Squeezing causes scarring.

## Post-acne marks
Dark marks after pimples fade slowly over months. Daily sunscreen is the most important step. Ingredients such as niacinamide, azelaic acid, vitamin C and tranexamic acid are commonly used. Do not pick spots, as it makes marks darker.

## When acne needs a doctor
Painful deep cysts or nodules, scarring, acne that covers large areas, or acne that does not improve after two to three months of consistent gentle care should be seen by a dermatologist. Prescription treatments should only be started under medical advice.
