# Skincare safety

## Combining strong actives
Do not use several strong actives together, especially on sensitive skin. Retinoids, exfoliating acids (AHA, BHA), benzoyl peroxide and high-strength vitamin C can cause dryness, burning and peeling when stacked. Use one new active at a time, a few nights per week at first.

## Prescription products
Products containing tretinoin, clindamycin, isotretinoin, hydroquinone or steroid creams are medicines. They should be started and monitored by a doctor, not chosen by an app.

## Signs you should see a professional
See a doctor or dermatologist for severe or persistent acne, significant swelling, severe irritation, a suspected allergic reaction, open wounds or bleeding skin, rapidly worsening symptoms, or any problem near the eyes.

## Pregnancy and breastfeeding
Retinoids and some acne medicines are not recommended in pregnancy. Anyone who is pregnant or breastfeeding should check skincare ingredients with a doctor.

## Community experiences
Experiences shared by people online are personal stories, not medical evidence. They can help you understand texture or feel of a product but do not prove that a product works for you.
