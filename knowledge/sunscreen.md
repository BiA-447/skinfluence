# Sunscreen

## Why sunscreen matters
Sunscreen is the most important daily step for preventing dark spots, post-acne marks and early ageing. Many brightening and exfoliating products make skin more sun sensitive, so sunscreen is needed when using them. Pakistan has strong UV for much of the year.

## SPF and PA ratings
SPF measures protection against UVB, which causes burning. The PA rating (PA+ to PA++++) shows UVA protection, which causes ageing and pigmentation. For daily use, SPF 30 to 50 with a good UVA rating is enough. Higher numbers add very little extra protection.

## How much and how often
Use about two finger-lengths of sunscreen for the face and neck. Reapply every two to three hours when outdoors for long periods. Indoor daytime use near windows still benefits from sunscreen.

## Choosing sunscreen by skin type
Oily and acne-prone skin usually prefers gel, fluid, milk or matte finishes. Dry skin usually prefers cream or hydrating sunscreens. Sensitive skin often tolerates mineral (zinc oxide or titanium dioxide) sunscreens well. Sun sticks are handy for reapplication.

## Mineral and chemical sunscreen
Mineral sunscreens sit on top of the skin and are often gentler for sensitive skin, but can leave a white cast. Chemical sunscreens absorb UV and usually feel lighter, but a few people find them stinging, especially near the eyes.
